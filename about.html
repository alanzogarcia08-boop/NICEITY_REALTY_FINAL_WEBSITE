Our Story

NiCeity Realty began with a simple belief: a home is more than a property. It is where families grow, celebrate, and rest. This belief is behind our tagline, "Where homes begin with heart."

For more than a decade, broker Ma. Cecilia Fermina R. Dela Cruz has guided buyers across Pampanga. Her clients include young families, overseas Filipino workers, and investors who wanted honest advice from someone they could trust.

We work with both ready-for-occupancy homes and pre-selling projects, so buyers can choose to move in soon or plan their purchase ahead of time.

Every client receives clear information and personal support, from the first inquiry until the day they receive their keys.

Who We Help

Families come to us when they are ready for more space, a safer neighborhood, or a home of their own. We help them compare options and find a house that fits both their needs and their budget.

Overseas Filipino workers often buy a home while they are away. We help them understand the process and keep them updated, so they can make decisions with confidence.

First-time homebuyers have many questions, and we are happy to answer all of them. Investors can also count on us for homes with strong potential in growing areas of Pampanga.
