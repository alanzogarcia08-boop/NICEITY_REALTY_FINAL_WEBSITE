Welcome to NiCeity Realty

NiCeity Realty is a Pampanga-based real estate brokerage founded by licensed broker Ma. Cecilia Fermina R. Dela Cruz. Since 2013, we have helped families, OFWs, first-time homebuyers, and investors find homes that fit their lifestyle and budget.

Our portfolio focuses on mid-range to luxury homes, including ready-for-occupancy (RFO) houses and pre-selling residential projects around Angeles City, San Fernando, and the Clark area.

Every property on this website is carefully selected, so you can spend less time searching and more time visiting homes you will love.

Whether you are buying your first home or looking for a property investment, we explain each step in plain language, from choosing a unit to understanding your financing options.

Where We Work

Our homes are found in some of the most in-demand areas of Pampanga. Angeles City is known for its busy shops, restaurants, and easy access to Clark. San Fernando is the provincial capital, with schools, hospitals, and government offices close by.

Homes near Clark are popular with working families and investors because of the nearby airport, business parks, and the freeport zone. Living in this area means shorter trips to work and easier travel.

Not sure which area suits you best? Tell us about your family, your work, and your budget, and we will help you choose.
