Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a house viewing?

Fill out the form above with your name, the unit you want to see, and your preferred time and date. We will confirm by email.

Can I visit on a Sunday?

Yes, but Sunday viewings are by appointment only. Please send your request early.

What payment options are available?

You can pay through bank financing or in cash. Both prices are listed on the Houses page.

Do you offer pre-selling projects?

Yes. We offer both ready-for-occupancy homes and pre-selling projects. Ask us about what is currently available.
