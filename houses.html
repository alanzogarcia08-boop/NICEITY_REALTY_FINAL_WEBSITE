Payment Options

Each unit lists two prices. Bank financing means a bank pays the seller and you repay the bank over time, usually through monthly payments. Cash means you pay the full amount directly, and the price is slightly lower.

Every home is sold as a fully furnished turnkey investment. This means the home is complete and ready for you to move in, rent out, or hold as an investment.

Not sure which option is better for you? Ask us during your viewing and we will go through the choices together.

Before You Book a Viewing

Make a short list of what matters most to you, such as the number of bedrooms, parking space, or how close the home is to work and school. This helps us show you the right units.
